2. How We Collect Info

DiaryBloom is designed to minimise the collection of personal information and protect the privacy of its users.

2.1 Personal Diary Information

DiaryBloom does not collect, access, or store users' private diary entries or associated journal content on servers operated by us. Such information is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to us or third-party service providers through the App's core journaling functionality.

Users are not required to create an account or provide personal identification information to use DiaryBloom's core journaling features.

2.2 Information Collected Through Third-Party Services

DiaryBloom uses third-party services to support certain App functionalities, including advertising and Premium subscription management. These service providers may collect or process limited information, such as device identifiers, technical information, advertising interactions, purchase records, and subscription status, depending on the services used and applicable privacy settings. The collection and processing of such information are subject to the respective service providers' privacy policies and applicable data protection laws.

2.3 Information Voluntarily Provided by Users

When users contact DiaryBloom for customer support or other enquiries, we may receive personal information voluntarily provided by them, including their email address, the contents of their communications, and any relevant subscription support identifiers. Such information is used for responding to enquiries, providing assistance, resolving technical issues, and fulfilling applicable legal obligations. Users are advised not to disclose private diary entries, passcodes, or other sensitive information in support communications unless necessary.

3. Local Data Storage and Security

3.1 Passcode Protection

DiaryBloom provides users with the option to enable a four-digit passcode to restrict access to the App. Passcode-related authentication information is protected using appropriate security mechanisms and is not transmitted to servers operated by DiaryBloom. The passcode provides an additional layer of access protection and does not replace the security measures provided by the user's device.

3.2 Device Backups and Data Recovery

DiaryBloom does not provide cloud-based synchronisation or remote recovery of diary entries. Diary information is intended to remain on the user's device and is not intentionally uploaded to external servers through DiaryBloom's core journaling functionality.

If the App is uninstalled, the device is lost or damaged, or the encryption keys become inaccessible, locally stored diary information may be permanently lost.

3.3 Exported Files and Third-Party Sharing

Where users choose to export diary entries as PDF files, the PDF files are generated locally on the user's device. Once users voluntarily export, save, email, or share these files through external applications or services, the handling of such files may be subject to the privacy and security practices of those third parties.

DiaryBloom does not control the storage, transmission, or subsequent use of exported files outside the App.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

8.1 Locally Stored Diary Information

Diary entries and related journal information are intended to remain stored locally on the user's device until deleted by the user or removed through an applicable device or application data-deletion process. Users may delete individual diary entries using the deletion functionality provided within the App. Uninstalling DiaryBloom may remove locally stored application data, subject to the device's operating system, backup configuration, and any copies previously exported or shared by the user. Users are encouraged to export any diary information they wish to preserve before uninstalling the App.

8.2 Subscription Information

Subscription-related information may be retained by RevenueCat, Apple, or Google in accordance with their respective retention policies and applicable legal obligations. Deleting the App does not automatically cancel an active subscription or necessarily delete subscription records maintained by those providers.

8.3 Customer Support Information

Personal information provided through customer support communications will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the relevant support purpose or comply with applicable legal, regulatory, or legitimate business requirements. When such information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymise, or otherwise dispose of it securely, where applicable.

We recognise the importance of protecting the confidentiality and integrity of personal diary information.

However, no electronic storage system or security measure can guarantee absolute protection against all security threats. We cannot guarantee that the App will be free from all vulnerabilities, unauthorised access, device compromise, or data loss. Users should maintain appropriate device security measures and exercise care when exporting or sharing personal information.
